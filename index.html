AusLegalAI for Windows

Early test copy, by invitation. Version 0.1.0.

Download for Windows
One file, 11 MB. Windows 10 or 11. No administrator password needed.

Installing

  1. Press Download for Windows. If the browser asks, choose Keep.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Windows shows a blue screen saying it protected your PC. Press More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the test copy is not yet signed.
  3. Press Next until it finishes. AusLegalAI opens, and its icon is now on the desktop and in the Start menu.
  4. Enter the firm's name, your name and the access key from your invitation email. AusLegalAI finds your client files itself.
AusLegalAI reads your client files and writes drafts for you to check. Nothing is sent or saved to your files until you press the button, and no file is ever replaced or deleted.
